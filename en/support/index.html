Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap two matching tiles to remove them. You can only use free tiles: nothing on top and one side, left or right, clear. You win when the table is clean. On the Home screen, tap “Settings” and then “How to play?” to repeat the guided tutorial whenever you like.

Why won't a tile respond?

It is blocked: it has another tile on top or tiles on both sides. With “Clarity” on, blocked tiles look darker.

What are the Destiny tiles?

They are the wildcards. Any Destiny pairs with another Destiny, even if the drawing is different.

I ran out of moves. Now what?

Use Shuffle to mix the remaining tiles, Undo to return your last pair, or restart the level, which costs nothing. Every board is dealt with a solution.

How do I earn coins?

By completing levels (more stars, more coins), opening the chest that appears every five levels and winning the Daily Challenge.

How do I earn three stars?

By finishing fast and using few helps. Hints and shuffles subtract stars; so does time.

How do I turn off sound or music?

On the Home screen, tap “Settings”: “Sound” and “Music” are there. Blue means on; gray means off.

What happens when I finish the first 20 levels?

The next world on the Map opens: there are three, with 20 levels each and ever-larger boards. After level 60 comes endless mode, with new boards generated by the game. You can also enter that mode earlier from the Map with the “Endless” button.

How do I change the language?

On Home, tap “Settings” and choose Español or English. If you never choose, the game uses Spanish on Spanish-language phones and English on all others.

Do I have to watch ads?

In the apps there is an ad strip during play and a full-screen ad every few levels. Ads that give coins or help only appear if you choose to watch them. The browser version has no ads.

Can I play without installing anything?

Yes. On this site's Play page the full game runs in the browser, on phone or computer. The first load downloads about 25 MB, so it is best done on wifi.

I lost my progress in the browser

The web version saves progress in the browser's storage. It is lost if you clear the site's data, and in a private or incognito window it is not kept when you close it. Web and app progress are independent.

I changed phones and lost my progress

Progress is saved only on the device, with no account or cloud copy, so it does not move from one phone to another and we cannot recover it.

Does the game use my data?

There are no accounts. Only if you play online duels do we store a random identifier, your nickname and your ranking points; we delete them if you ask. In the apps, Google AdMob ads use data from the device; the details are in the privacy policy.
