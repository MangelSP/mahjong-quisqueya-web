Accessibility

What Mahjong Quisqueya does so more people can play it, and what it still lacks.

What the game already offers

  • No forced rush. No level is lost because of time: the clock only decides how many stars you earn.
  • One tap at a time. Everything is played with simple taps; there are no gestures, no dragging and nothing to press and hold.
  • Large buttons. Everything you tap is at least 44 points on each side.
  • Not dependent on color. Each tile is recognized by its drawing, and blocked tiles are told apart by brightness, not hue. Clarity mode darkens them and can be turned off.
  • Not dependent on sound. Every audio cue has a visual one, and sound and music can be turned off separately in Settings.
  • Help so you never get stuck. Hint, Undo and Shuffle, and a tutorial that points to where to tap.
  • Two languages. Spanish and English, selectable in Settings.
  • No flashing. The game does not use rapid flashes or sudden changes of light.

What it does not have yet

  • It is not compatible with screen readers (VoiceOver, TalkBack).
  • Text size does not follow the system's large text setting.
  • There is no option to reduce animations.
  • It can only be played in portrait.

We say it plainly so you know what to expect before downloading. These are improvements we want to make.
