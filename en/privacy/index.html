Privacy policy

What data Mahjong Quisqueya uses, who receives it and what you can decide. No fine print.

In short: the game asks for no account and your progress lives only on your device. The only thing that reaches a server of ours is what online duels need, and only if you play them: a random identifier, your nickname, your progress in the race and your leaderboard points. The iPhone and Android apps show Google AdMob ads, which do use data from your device to display them; the browser version has no ads.

Who is responsible for the game

Mahjong Quisqueya is a game by Miguel Angel Sanchez Peralta. For any privacy matter you can write to corewave.solutions.tech@gmail.com.

Data we collect ourselves

Only what online duels need, and only if you use them. The game does not ask for your name, email, phone, contacts or photos, and your campaign progress, coins and settings never leave your device. Everything else (campaign, Daily Challenge, endless mode and the duel against the bot) works without talking to our server.

Online duels

In the iPhone and Android apps you can race against other people. To put you in a room together and show who is ahead, the game connects to a server we run and sends it:

  • a random identifier the game creates on your device the first time you enter an online duel. It is not your name, your email or the advertising identifier, and it cannot be used to find out who you are;
  • your nickname, which the game generates by itself (for example, "Jugador 1234") or which you choose, and which the others in your room and anyone viewing the leaderboard can see. The server rejects offensive nicknames;
  • race data: the room you are in, how many pairs you have left and where you finished;
  • your global leaderboard points, which the server adds up at the end of each race;
  • room chat messages, which can only be the game's fixed phrases and emojis: free text cannot be typed.

As with any internet connection, the server sees the IP address you connect from. The connection is encrypted and goes through Cloudflare, which protects the server and handles that technical data under its privacy policy.

We use this data only to make the duel work. We do not use it for advertising, sell it or share it with anyone else. Race data and chat messages are not stored: they are discarded when the race ends or the room closes. The random identifier, the nickname and your leaderboard points stay on the server so it can recognise you next time and show the leaderboard. If you want us to delete them, write to us and we will explain how to tell us which one is yours.

If you never enter an online duel, the game does not create that identifier or connect to our server.

What is stored on your device

So you can continue where you left off, the game stores in the app's own storage (or, in the web version, in your browser's local storage):

  • the levels you have won, with their stars and your best score;
  • your coins and the tile sets you have unlocked;
  • your settings: language, sound, music, tile set in use and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is erased when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data; in the web version, when you clear this site's data in your browser.

Ads in the apps

The iPhone and Android apps are supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. There are three kinds: a strip during play, a full-screen ad every few levels, and rewarded ads, which are shown only if you choose to watch them in exchange for coins or help.

To show and measure those ads, AdMob may collect directly from your device:

  • the device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android);
  • the IP address, from which an approximate location is inferred (country or city, never your exact location);
  • device and app data: model, operating system, language, game version;
  • your interaction with ads and diagnostic data, such as crashes and performance.

Google uses this data to show ads (personalized only if you allow it), limit how often you see the same one, measure results and prevent fraud. We do not receive that data: we only see overall figures, such as how many ads were shown. Google explains how it handles it in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in its privacy policy.

What you can decide

  • On iPhone: the system asks whether you allow tracking. If you say no, you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: when you open the app you are asked for consent to personalized ads, and you can change your answer at any time.
  • Rewarded ads: they are always optional; the whole game can be played without watching them.

Permissions

The game does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or access to your files. On iPhone, the only system prompt is the ad tracking one.

Purchases

The iPhone and Android apps offer optional in-game purchases: removing ads, power packs and coin bundles. Payment is processed by Apple or Google with your store account: we never see or store your card or payment details. The game only receives from the store the confirmation of which product you bought, in order to give it to you. Coins can also be earned by playing or by watching rewarded ads, and the whole game can be played without buying anything.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will deal with it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. The only thing we store ourselves is the random identifier, nickname and points for online duels, which we delete if you ask. Everything else is on your device (erased by uninstalling the game) or with Google, which offers its controls at My Ad Center. If you need help exercising a right, write to us.

This website

This site uses no cookies, forms, ads or analytics. The web version of the game is downloaded from this same site and runs in your browser: once loaded it sends and receives nothing else. Its progress is saved in the browser's local storage, not in cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub, like any web server, may log technical data about the visit, such as the IP address. GitHub explains this in its privacy statement.

If something changes

If a future version adds accounts or any other feature that uses data, we will update this page before releasing it and say so on the game's store listing.
